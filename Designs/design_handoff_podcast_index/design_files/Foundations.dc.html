Score bands

All seven sit at lightness 0.66 to 0.87 with chroma held between 0.13 and 0.16, so they read as one family and always take near-black text. The brand red sits far below them at lightness 0.60 with chroma 0.225: darker, more saturated, and always paired with white text. That difference in weight is what stops garbage red and brand red from being confused.
