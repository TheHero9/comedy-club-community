Why 5 x 2 rather than a slider or a row of ten. Ten 44px targets need 440px, so a single row cannot exist at 390. A slider turns a discrete ten-point judgement into a drag, which is imprecise with a thumb and gives no target for the value you already know you want. Two rows of five keeps every value one tap away, keeps the numbers legible at 20px, and leaves room under each for its band stripe, so the score you are about to give is visibly connected to the colour it will take in the grid.
