Why the grid transposes on mobile

The problem with the desktop orientation at 390px

Years as rows and episodes as columns makes a 2,100px strip for three years. To reach episode 30 of 2025 you scroll sideways past 29 cells with no landmarks, the sticky year column eats 90px of a 390px screen, and cells have to shrink to 56x36 to feel worth scrolling, which fails the 44px target. Three markers then have to stack in the corners of a chip smaller than a fingertip.

What transposing buys

Episodes as rows and years as columns turns the wide axis into the short one. Three years is three columns of about 100px, so the grid fits with no horizontal scroll at all, and the long axis becomes ordinary vertical page scroll. Cells get 44px of height and enough width for the score plus all three markers in a row. At eight channels a year with more columns, the cell area scrolls inside its container while the episode number column stays pinned left, so the rule still holds: the page never moves sideways.

TOUCH TARGET
Mobile cell is 100 x 44. Desktop cell is 54 x 44 and stays pointer-driven.
PAYLOAD FIT
The API is episode-major already: rows[episodeIndex].cells[seasonIndex]. Mobile renders it untransposed, desktop transposes it.
SCROLL RULE
The cell area is the only overflow-x container on the page. Body stays overflow-x hidden.
